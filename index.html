Privacy Policy — Pause

Last updated 6 October 2026

Pause does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. It does not declare the INTERNET permission, so it has no technical ability to send anything anywhere.

What Pause reads on your device

To do its job, Pause reads the following. All of it stays on your phone, in local app storage:

  • The list of apps installed on your device, so it can show them and let you launch them.
  • Daily usage totals and app open counts, read from Android's own usage statistics, so it can show you your screen time.
  • Your own settings: which apps you marked as distracting, your quiet hours, your chosen wallpaper.
  • Notification content, only if you turn on notification access. Pause mirrors notifications into its own inbox so you can read them in one place. It never cancels or alters the originals.

Sleep sounds

At bedtime Pause offers a few calm sounds to fall asleep to. They are ordinary audio files bundled inside the app. Playing one reads nothing about you, records nothing, and sends nothing: Pause has no microphone access and makes no recordings of any kind. A sound fades out and stops on its own after 45 minutes.

Permissions

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — required for a launcher to list and start every app on the device.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS — optional, granted by you in Settings, used only to display your screen time.
  • Notification access — optional, granted by you in Settings, used only for the in-app notification inbox.
  • SET_WALLPAPER — used only when you pick a wallpaper inside Pause.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — used only to keep a sleep sound playing once the screen goes off. Android requires a foreground service for audio that continues after you stop looking at the phone.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional. Pause asks the first time you start a sleep sound, and uses it only to show that sound’s ongoing notification with a Stop button. Decline it and the sound still plays; you stop it from the Good night screen instead.

Pause has no INTERNET permission, no location access, no camera or microphone access, and no contacts access.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Pause stores lives in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is backed up to us, because there is no us to back it up to. Uninstalling Pause deletes all of it permanently.

Children

Pause is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
